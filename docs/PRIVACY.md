# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Netra measures your connection without accounts, ads, or analytics. Your results stay on your device.

## What leaves your device

| Destination | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `speed.cloudflare.com` | Test traffic: downloaded data, random uploaded data, and small ping requests | Measuring speed and latency. Like any web server, Cloudflare sees your IP address |
| `api.ipify.org` | One request | Showing your public IP address on the test screen |

Nothing else is sent. Netra has no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs, and no server of its own.

## What stays on your device

- **Test history:** speeds, ping, jitter, latency under load, the speed chart, the connection type, and the
  Wi-Fi or carrier name at the time of the test. It's stored in the app's private storage. You can delete one
  result or all of them in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes everything.
- **Settings:** test duration, parallel connections, unit, and theme.
- Android may include this data in your device backup, which you control in system settings.

## Location permission

Android shares the Wi-Fi name (SSID) only with apps that have precise location access. Netra asks for location
**only** to show and label results with the Wi-Fi name. It never reads or stores your location (GPS
coordinates). You can deny the permission and every measurement still works; the Wi-Fi name is then hidden.

## Sharing results

When you share a result, Netra creates an image on your device and hands it to the app you choose. The image
contains the date, connection type, speeds, ping, and bufferbloat grade. It **never** includes the Wi-Fi name
or your IP address.
